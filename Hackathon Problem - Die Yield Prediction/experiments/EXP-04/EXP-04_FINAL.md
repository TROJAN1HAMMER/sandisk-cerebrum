# EXP-04 — Independent Holdout Generalization & Final Model Validation

## Executive Summary

**EXP-04** is the definitive validation experiment for the SanDisk Cerebrum Die Yield Prediction system.

Across preceding experiments:
- **EXP-01** restored true wafer-level spatial defect densities (`density_3x3_restored`, `density_5x5_restored`), enhancing Model A screening ranking.
- **EXP-02** mapped the asymmetric decision space, demonstrating that threshold $\tau_A = 0.5100$ and preferred cascade gate $[0.295, 0.550]$ reduce false alarms and computational cost by over 94%.
- **EXP-03 / EXP-03B** engineered and froze 10 high-resolution sub-die block anomaly features (`cnt_110`, `cluster_area_110`, `max_roll20`, etc.), proving that **B2 (Enhanced Model B)** was the superior stage-2 candidate.

In EXP-04, the complete, confirmed B2 cascade was deployed **under strict freeze** on an independent population of **40 unseen wafers (32,598 eligible dies, 1,380 post-test defects)** from `input/test.csv`. Zero model fitting, zero hyperparameter searches, and zero threshold sweeps were permitted.

### Core Empirical Findings
1. **Generalization Confirmation**:
   - The frozen B2 cascade achieved **F1 = 0.5177** (95% CI: $[0.4948, 0.5369]$), **F2 = 0.4190**, **MCC = 0.5517**, **PR-AUC = 0.4808**, and **ROC-AUC = 0.8078**.
   - The generalization gap between the canonical holdout (EXP-03B: $F1 = 0.5254$) and the independent holdout is **only -0.0077 (-1.47% relative)**. 
   - Both PR-AUC (+0.65% rel) and ROC-AUC (+1.16% rel) improved on the independent population.
2. **True Incremental Value Over Model A**:
   - Model A alone achieved $F1 = 0.5005, \text{Recall} = 34.49\%, TP = 476$.
   - The B2 cascade boosted F1 to **0.5177 (+3.44% relative)** and Recall to **37.17% (+7.77% relative)**, capturing **513 True Positives** (+37 net defects recovered) while bypassing **93.78% of dies** from expensive block parsing.
3. **Rescue Forensics**:
   - Out of 176 Model A false negatives escalated into Gate B, B2 rescued **41 dies (23.30% rescue rate)**.
   - Out of 21 Model A true positives escalated, B2 preserved 17 (81.0% preservation, only 4 suppressed).
   - This yielded a **net defect gain of +37 dies** with 89 total cascade false positives (0.28% FPR).
4. **Per-Wafer Stability**:
   - Across 40 independent wafers, the cascade outperformed Model A on **20 wafers**, tied on **6 wafers**, and underperformed on **14 wafers**, raising the mean wafer F1 from 0.4511 to **0.4629** and median wafer F1 from 0.4963 to **0.5058**.
5. **Final Status**:
   - Classified as **FINAL VALIDATED CANDIDATE**.

```text

         │
         ▼

         │
         ▼
Model A Inference (P_A computed for 100% of dies)
         │
         ├───────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────┐

                                                     │
                                                     ▼
                                        Model B B2 Inference (P_B)
                                                     │
                                        ┌────────────┴────────────┐
                                        ▼                         ▼
                                   P_B < 0.5376              P_B ≥ 0.5376
                                  (1,894 dies)                (134 dies)
```

## Limitations

1. **Marginal Defects in Fast Pass**: In the fast-pass band ($P_A < 0.295$), 728 latent defects were bypassed because their parametric electrical features did not trigger Model A. Rescuing these requires lowering the gate lower bound or developing a lightweight sub-die screening heuristic.
2. **Static Thresholding Across Lots**: Thresholds $\tau_A = 0.5100$ and $\tau_B = 0.5376$ were held strictly fixed. In high-defect fabrication lots, dynamic lot-adaptive thresholding could yield additional recall.
